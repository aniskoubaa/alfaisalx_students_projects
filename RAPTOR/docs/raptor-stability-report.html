Does the live demo survive?

You asked for the new camera at high resolution and frame rate, video tests (not just images) aimed at the “crashes when something shows up suddenly” problem, and a model change if it kept failing. Here is what was measured on the Jetson, what was fixed, and what is still unknown.

Camera1080p @ 60

Arducam B0498 on USB 3. Delivered exactly 60.0 fps through the demo’s capture thread.

Crashes0 of 6 real runs

Video, live camera, windowed, 3–16 min each. None crashed, before or after the fixes.

Fault injection3 of 3 pass

Failures forced on purpose: demo survives the small ones, stops cleanly on the big one.

Model changeNot needed

No model fault found. YOLO26s beat RT-DETRv4-S on steadiness and speed.

The honest caveat

The crash you saw was not reproduced. I can say what I tried and what I fixed, not what caused it. The likeliest cause is the old webcam dropping off USB (documented 29 Sept), which this USB 3 camera hasn’t done — an inference, not a proof. What is now guaranteed is that if it happens again, a log will say where.

1. The camera

The Arducam gives uncompressed YUYV only (no MJPG). That is fine on USB 3: no decoding, 1080p60 costs about one CPU core.

Bar = frames per second delivered through live_camera.py. 4K exists but only at 15 fps. The old C922 managed 1080p at 30 fps on USB 2.

Two suspected causes that did NOT reproduce

A thread race on the VLM answers (0 errors in 38,750 tries) and degenerate zero-height boxes through the tracker (no exception). Neither is claimed as the cause.

4. Should the model change?

The trigger for changing models was a model-caused crash or inconsistency. None was found, but the deployed Apache-2.0 alternative was compared anyway, on real continuous video (795 frames).

Decision

Keep YOLO26s. Same people found, steadier, a third faster. Note the bench model sees 1.2 people per frame on the same aerial video against 6.2: it is for desk demos only and must never be used to judge detection from altitude.
